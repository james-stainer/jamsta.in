PhD thesis

Forecasting vital signs in paediatric intensive care

UCL and Great Ormond Street Hospital, 2021 to 2026. Viva passed October 2026 with minor corrections.

I used a PyTorch StemGNN model to forecast heart rate and blood pressure together from 5-second bedside-monitor data, treating the dependency between them as a learned graph. The pipeline behind it takes 4,120 raw admissions down to 364 high-quality patients, with age-aware normalisation, missing-data handling and outlier detection.
