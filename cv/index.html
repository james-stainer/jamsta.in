Selected Projects

Multivariate ICU Forecasting with StemGNN

PhD Thesis at UCL & Great Ormond Street Hospital
  • Incorporated a PyTorch StemGNN model to jointly forecast heart rate and blood pressure from 5-second paediatric ICU bedside-monitor data at Great Ormond Street Hospital.
  • Built the full clinical time-series pipeline: filtering from 4,120 raw admissions to 364 high-quality patients, age-aware normalisation, missing-data handling, outlier detection, and sliding-window segmentation across ~12.5M observations.
  • Modelled HR–BP dependencies as a learned graph and benchmarked performance against baselines.
  • Achieved 2.30% / 3.49% MAPE (HR / BP) on 5-minute-ahead forecasts at 1-minute resolution, outperforming ARIMA (2.82% / 3.85%) and a persistence baseline.

DrugnomeAI – Gene Druggability Prediction

GitHub | Web App
  • Published ML framework predicting druggability across all 19,846 human protein-coding genes; integrated 324 features from 15 sources and outperformed three published benchmarks.
  • Developed a stochastic semi-supervised ML framework that scores druggability likelihood for all protein-coding genes in the human exome.
  • Top-ranked genes were significantly enriched for targets already in clinical development (p < 1 × 10−308) and for genome-wide significant hits in UK Biobank PheWAS of 450K exomes.
  • Built specialised models stratified by drug modality (small molecule, antibody, PROTAC) and therapeutic area, outperforming three published benchmarks on independent validation sets.
